**自己账户之间转账的重复识别。** 仍是 pre-alpha：核心可用且经过测试，不要把唯一的账放在它上面。 / **Spotting the same transfer on both of your own accounts.** Still pre-alpha: the core works and is tested, but don't keep your only copy of your books in it.

## 新增 · What is new

- **转账的两侧 · The two sides of one transfer**：银行账单里付出 500、微信账单里收入 500，是同一笔转账。现在 `money-mom dupes` 会把它们列为候选（`kind: "transfer"`）：金额互为相反数、账户不同、来源不同、日期接近；一边点名了另一边的账户或带转账、充值、提现、还款字样时列为 `likely`；没有理由时，只有一边还没分类才会问，已经被你的规则分好类的不打扰你。 / Opposite amounts in two accounts, different sources, close dates; `likely` when one side names the other account or says transfer, top-up, withdrawal or repayment. Without a reason it only asks while one side is unclassified.
- **你说了算 · You decide**：`money-mom dupes resolve ID1 ID2 --transfer` 作废两行（不删除），换成一笔真正的转账，日期取钱离开的那天；重新导入这两份账单它们也不会回来。AI 必须转述你的回答（`--user-said`）。导入时像另一侧的行同样先挂起，不计入任何余额。 / Both rows are voided (nothing is deleted) and one real transfer takes their place, dated when the money left; re-importing does not bring them back. An AI must relay your answer; on import such a row is held, in no balance.
- 测试 585 个，另有对每条新规则的变异测试。 / 585 tests, plus mutation checks of every new rule.

## 已知限制 · Known limitations

- 写入了 `chain` 字段和 `review` 事件的账本，0.1.0a4 及更早版本读不了 · Ledgers with `chain` fields and `review` events cannot be read by 0.1.0a4 or earlier
- 哈希链只能发现改动，不能阻止；重算了此后所有哈希的改写发现不了 · The hash chain detects, it does not prevent
- 支付宝映射仍是未经真实导出验证的草案；微信映射只核对过一份导出 · The Alipay mapping is still an unverified draft; the WeChat one was checked against a single export
- 没有内置银行预设；各 agent 内的对话效果还没有逐一实测 · No built-in bank presets; behaviour inside each agent has not been tested one by one
