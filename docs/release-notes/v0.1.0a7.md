## 新增 · What is new

- **成本批次 · Cost lots**：买入建立一个批次（份额、买入价、日期），卖出按先进先出（`--lots lifo|hifo` 可选）从批次里拿；成本和已实现盈亏写进同一笔分录，不事后估算。卖出超过持仓、或作废一笔其份额已被卖出的买入，都会被拒绝。 / A purchase creates a lot; a sale takes from lots first in first out (or `--lots lifo|hifo`), and the cost and the realised gain are written into the entry. Selling more than is held, or voiding a purchase whose shares were sold, is refused.
- **命令 · Commands**：`invest`、`buy`（`--opening` 引入已持有的份额）、`sell`、`dividend`、`holdings`、`pnl`。手续费与预扣税是费用，不计入成本。币种必须用 `--ccy` 说明，从不猜。 / Fees and withheld tax are expenses, not part of the cost. The currency is always stated, never guessed.
- **价格从不编造 · Prices are never made up**：`holdings` 依次用 `--mark`、你记下的价格、最后一笔成交价（并标明不是行情）；没有价格的持仓单独列出，总数标为不完整，**不当作零**。程序不联网取股价。 / No price means listed unpriced and the totals partial, never zero. The program does not go online for share prices.
- **读取 TradeGit 日志 · TradeGit journal import**：`trades import`，只读。Money Mom 不自己解析券商文件（没有真实样本的解析器就是猜），读取 TradeGit 规范化后的日志（含更正与作废）；其他券商由 AI 读账单后以 JSON 行提交。期权、卖空、零价事件、调整**不会被猜**，逐条列出；卖出超过账本持仓会让整批停下并指出哪一行。重复导入安全。 / Read-only. Options, shorts, expiries and adjustments are listed, never guessed; a sale of shares the ledger does not hold stops the batch and names the row.
- **逐账户净资产 · Net worth by account**：`networth --by-account`，每个账户折算成一种货币，各占资产多少，未定价的持仓使该账户标为不完整。 / Each account converted, with its share of the assets; an unpriced holding marks its account partial.
- **台币等 · TWD and others**：`rates update` 对欧洲央行没有的币种向第二个来源（open.er-api.com）询问最新汇率，每条标明来源并附署名；`--no-fallback` 关闭。仍然只发送币种代码。 / A second source is asked only for currencies the ECB does not publish, and named on every line; `--no-fallback` turns it off.
- 测试 631 个，另有对每条新规则的变异测试。 / 631 tests, plus mutation checks of every new rule.

## 联网 · The network

程序仍然只有一个会联网的命令：`money-mom rates update`（取汇率，只发送币种代码和日期）。这个版本里，欧洲央行没有的币种会向第二个来源询问，可以用 `--no-fallback` 关闭。 · Still only one command uses the network: `money-mom rates update`. Currencies the ECB does not publish are asked of a second source, which `--no-fallback` turns off.

## 已知限制 · Known limitations

- 不支持卖空、期权与期货、拆股与分拆、平均成本法 · No short selling, options and futures, splits and spin-offs, or average-cost accounting
- 写入了成本批次的账本，0.1.0a6 及更早版本读不了 · Ledgers with cost lots cannot be read by 0.1.0a6 or earlier
- 投资功能没有用真实券商账户做过端到端验证：测试用的是按文档格式写的合成日志 · The investing features were tested with synthetic journals written from TradeGit's documented format, not end to end with a real broker account
- 支付宝映射仍是未经真实导出验证的草案；微信映射只核对过一份导出 · The Alipay mapping is still an unverified draft; the WeChat one was checked against a single export
